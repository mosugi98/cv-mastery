Practice in the simulator

Nine emergency cardiovascular cases you can play: read the vitals, examine the patient, order tests and give treatment. Learning mode lets you take your time. Challenge mode runs in real time.

The simulator opens in its own page and has a link back here. It needs an internet connection.

    Educational use only. The simulator's clinical content has not been clinically reviewed and is not for patient care. Your simulator scores stay in your own browser and are not part of the study progress backup.
